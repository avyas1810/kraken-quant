## Reconciled findings

The frozen result is **0.3716717** Sharpe from 4-hour net returns, sample standard deviation, zero risk-free return and `sqrt(365 × 6) = sqrt(2190)` annualisation. The inspected OOS contains 5,448 4-hour returns. Compounding six consecutive UTC bars into each of 908 daily returns produces Sharpe **0.2998513** under Eric's `sqrt(252)` convention and **0.3608711** under `sqrt(365)` for a 24/7 market. Total compounded return remains **+12.0116096%**, with wealth difference `5.8e-15`. The daily and 4-hour Sharpe values differ because daily compounding and serial dependence change the sample mean/volatility ratio. None of these calculations turns the inspected period into unseen validation.

The available authentic historical universe evidence is now stronger than the earlier logs: the official Binance archive yielded a cohort of the top 30 non-fiat/non-stable/non-leveraged USDT base assets by August 2021 quote volume, selected before the October 2021 start. The 30-asset low-correlation strategy has training Sharpe **-1.5461** and already-inspected OOS Sharpe **-1.4519**, net return **-43.95%**, and maximum drawdown **-44.97%**, under both the -99.9% terminal-loss and 0% additional terminal-loss assumptions. The unfiltered 30-asset OOS Sharpe ranges from **-1.8367** to **-2.2525** across those assumptions. The expanded cohort therefore improves relative to a weak baseline but does not establish alpha. This is a static inception cohort, not a rolling top-30 selection. Selection and price/liquidity evidence are genuine and checksum-verified; archive first/last bars and first unexplained gaps remain proxies for exact exchange-effective listing, delisting, migration and recovery events. OOS remains inspected.

Frozen accounting uses gross traded notional divided by beginning equity, counting buy and sell legs, and charges 20 bp of that notional. It is not half the absolute weight change. The replay verifies `gross return - net return = 0.002 × turnover` to floating-point tolerance. Signal weights use a completed close and are applied at that close before the next close-to-close return; this is causal in bar indexing but assumes an unattainable instantaneous same-close fill. Extra execution stress is sensitivity analysis, not calibrated market impact or an order-book simulator.

The BTC regression has beta approximately **-0.0113**, R² **0.00120**, and net arithmetic annual intercept approximately **+6.04%**; HAC p-values are **0.566–0.601** and confidence intervals include zero. It aligns 5,448 observations without drop/fill. The intercept is not a full CAPM excess-return alpha: the regression uses a BTC factor and the study has no risk-free series.

Mark's exact “same share of time” placebo is fulfilled: both null generators preserve the active fraction exactly and re-run the frozen accounting. Run-length and circular-shift unconditional Sharpe p-values are 0.027 and 0.018; after predeclared post-hoc exposure ±5% and turnover ±10% balance conditions they are 0.02887 and 0.02429, with 761 and 740 accepted of 999. The nulls do not match active dates to contemporaneous market covariates. These conditional randomization results are unadjusted for unknown historical experiments and do not establish standalone alpha.

The current empirical finding remains a modest relative improvement, not established standalone profitability: the corrected inspected OOS 4-hour result is 0.3717 Sharpe, +12.0116% cumulative return and -30.0367% maximum drawdown. Absolute block-bootstrap Sharpe intervals cross zero. The old 0.75 headline is superseded.

Authentic 30-asset point-in-time cohort selection, quote liquidity and 4-hour prices are now available and empirically tested. Exact exchange-effective listing/delisting notices, token migration/corporate-action mapping and delisting recovery values remain unverified; the replay therefore uses first-observation/first-gap proxies and explicit terminal sensitivities. Historical trial history is incomplete: 18 correlated trials are reconstructed, but unknown experiments are not invented. OOS is already inspected. Candidate registration history was overwritten during implementation; the reconstructed record is invalid as contemporaneous preregistration. Genuinely future unseen evaluation is still required.
